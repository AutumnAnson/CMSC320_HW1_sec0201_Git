Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines changes from one branch into another and creates 
a merge commit when necessary.  Rebasing moves commits from one branch onto 
another branch, creating a more linear history.  Merging preserves branch hist,
rebasing creates a cleaner history.
